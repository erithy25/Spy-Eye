Timing: Germany’s digital operating picture

Picture

“The federal government is developing with the states a joint digital domestic operating picture for civil protection, so that everyone responsible has a uniform basis of information for their operations, digitally and in real time.”

BMI, Eckpunkte paper,
20 May 2026, p. 5

Window

In 2026 “a first pilot application was successfully developed”. In 2027 the decision on joint live operation by the federal government and the states is due.

Bundestag printed paper
21/7120, answer 56

Budget

Ten billion euros for civil protection by 2029: funds already budgeted, “not a grant programme”. The buyers are the federal agencies BBK and THW.

BMI, Eckpunkte, p. 3
Bundestag paper 21/7126

The call for the operating picture is in a government paper. A call for image search within it is nowhere.

Quotations: our translation. BMI: Federal Ministry of the Interior.

SICHT

Problem

The German federal government is building a digital operating picture together with the states, and in 2027 the decision on live operation is due. Whoever is not known as a supplier by then will not get into the first stage. The ten billion euros are funds already budgeted, not a pot one applies to. And frankly: no authority has so far asked for image search in the operating picture. BMI is the Federal Ministry of the Interior, BBK the Federal Office of Civil Protection and Disaster Assistance, THW the Federal Agency for Technical Relief. The quotations are our translation from German.
