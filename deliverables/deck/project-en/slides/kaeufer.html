Buyers: the path to the first trial

Federal

BBK with the Joint Information and Situation Centre; THW with 669 local sections.

Before the 2027 decision no one there holds the requirement.

States

Interior ministries and state agencies with their situation centres.

Decide state-wide and are in part already committed.

Local

400 counties and independent cities as lower disaster control authorities.

Small budgets, a data protection review per county.

Step 1

A paid trial at a county with its own drone unit: three months, one exercise, one measurement report.

Step 2

A second trial in another state.

Step 3

Then the state: framework and federation.

Stop criterion

If no authority commits to a trial by the end of March 2027, the demand thesis counts as not confirmed.

Sources: THW · Federal Statistical Office via Deutscher Städtetag · Bundestag printed paper 21/7126, answer 28.

SICHT

Market

There are buyers at three levels of the German state. The realistic path starts from below: a paid trial in an exercise at a county with its own drone unit, with a measurement report as the result. Then a second state, then a framework agreement. I have set myself a stop criterion: if no authority commits to a trial by the end of March 2027, the demand thesis is wrong. BBK is the Federal Office of Civil Protection and Disaster Assistance, THW the Federal Agency for Technical Relief.
