Ahr valley, Germany · 14 July 2021

22:14

Until 22:43 a police helicopter filmed the villages from Mayschoß to Schuld: houses flooded up to the attic, and people signalling with lamps.

The videos were “not properly filed”. Until September 2022 the parliamentary inquiry and the public prosecutors assumed that no such footage existed.

Toll

At least 135 dead in the Ahr valley.

Not documented

Whether the footage reached the incident command.

Sources: dpa via Volksstimme, 5 Oct 2022 · epd via evangelisch.de, 4 Oct 2022 · dpa via Deutsches Ärzteblatt, 2 Aug 2024. Quotation: our translation.

I pin the problem to this case, the flood in the Ahr valley in Germany in July 2021. It is documented that the helicopter filmed and that the videos were not properly filed; the parliamentary inquiry and the prosecutors knew nothing of them until September 2022. No source shows whether the footage reached the incident command, so I do not claim it. The footage existed; an operating picture made from it did not.
