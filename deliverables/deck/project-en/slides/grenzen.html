Limits: what Sicht is not meant to do

01

No face recognition.

No matching against images of persons. Faces are masked in every frame.

02

No licence plates.

They are neither read nor searched.

03

No finding again, no tracking.

Queries for particular persons are refused. Sex, age and clothing only under a mandate that permits them.

04

No protected attributes.

No search by origin, religion, health, hair colour or tattoos.

05

No central data pool.

Footage stays with the authority that recorded it.

06

No silent learning.

Retraining is a separate, recorded step.

Partly architecture, partly rule with a ledger. The query check is a check of words and can be circumvented by paraphrase; that is what the ledger and the sample are for.

SICHT

Evidence

These limits are a selling point, not a concession: no faces, no licence plates, no search by origin or bodily features, no central data pool. I am precise about it: some of it is architecture, for instance that there is no model for faces and that recordings do not leave the node. The rest is a rule with a ledger, for instance the query check, and that is what I call it.
