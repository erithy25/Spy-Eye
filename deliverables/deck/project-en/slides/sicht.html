What Sicht is

Sicht makes the video that authorities record during operations anyway searchable within seconds and watches it for hazards on its own.

Without identifying people, without pooling data, and with a ledger that accounts for every query.

The measurable purpose: time from recording to awareness.

Detection delay

Seconds from the first image of an event to the alert on the situation map.

Coverage

Share of the recordings received that have been analysed in full.

Recorded queries

An answer is delivered only after the ledger entry has been written.

Product
