Pattern: two more documented cases

Los Angeles · January 2025

First responders and incident commanders were “unable to consistently share real-time information”, among other things because of “various unconnected platforms”.

County of Los Angeles, McChrystal After-Action Report, highlights, 25 Sep 2025

Maui · 8 August 2023

At least 102 dead. Released afterwards: “approximately 850 gigabytes of video files, audio files, photographs and documents”, among them police body camera footage.

Hawaii Department of the Attorney General, News Release 2024-40, 13 Sep 2024 · Maui Now, 13 Sep 2024

What the records support

Footage is produced during operations and often does not reach those who decide, or reaches them too late.

What they do not support

That searchable video would have changed the course of any of these events. Software does not fix organisational failure.

SICHT

Problem

Two more officially documented cases. In Los Angeles the county’s report records that real-time information could not be shared consistently; on Maui about 850 gigabytes of material were released afterwards. I explicitly do not say that software would have changed the course of either. I say: the path from image to awareness is the weak point.
