SICHT
Sicht, German for sight · the image layer for the civil operating picture

No sight without oversight.

Sicht makes the video that authorities record during operations anyway searchable within seconds and watches it for hazards on its own: without identifying people, without pooling data, and with a ledger that accounts for every query.

The problem

Ahr valley, Germany, 14 July 2021, 22:14 to 22:43. A police helicopter films flooded villages and people signalling with lamps. The videos are “not properly filed”. The parliamentary inquiry and the public prosecutors learn of them only in September 2022.1

“So the technical incident command did not know what was still to come in the course of the evening.”2

No source shows that searchable footage would have changed the course of that night. What is documented: the footage existed, was not properly recorded and remained unknown for more than a year. Quotations translated from German.

What Sicht does

Einsicht
Insight. Free-text search across all of an authority’s footage, in German or English: “flooded meadow”, “smoke above buildings”.
Vorsicht
Caution. Standing queries report hazards to the staff with frame, place and time. A person confirms or dismisses.
Weitsicht
Foresight. Between authorities only the question travels. The image stays where it was recorded.
Aufsicht
Oversight. Every query has a purpose, a responsible person and an entry in a chained ledger that can be verified.

The measurable purpose: time from recording to awareness

Detection delaySeconds from an event appearing in the image to the alert on the map. Measured in every exercise, per event.
CoverageShare of the recordings received that have been analysed in full.
Recorded queriesNumber of queries and of refusals. An answer is delivered only after the ledger entry has been written.

What Sicht cannot do, by design

  • No face recognition, no matching against images of persons. Detected faces are masked.
  • No licence plates: neither read nor searched.
  • No finding again and no tracking of particular persons. Descriptions by sex, age or clothing only under a mandate that permits them: a rule with a ledger, not a technical impossibility.
  • No search by name, origin, religion, health or bodily features; no scoring of persons.
  • No central data pool: footage stays with the authority that recorded it.
  • No silent learning: retraining is a separate, recorded step.

Status
Trial build. Exercise data. No government customers.

Today: video files and exercise operation, a federation of three nodes in the demonstration. Live streams from cameras: built and tested in the lab, not tried in the field; alerts follow about half a minute after the event. Not built yet: the coupling to existing command and control systems. Runs on the authority’s own machines, without a network connection.
