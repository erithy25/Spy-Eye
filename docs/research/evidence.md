# Evidence file for the "Erda" pitch

Compiled 2026-11-18. Web search and page/PDF fetch only; no modelling, no extrapolation.

## How to read this file

Each bullet has the form: **claim — figure/quote — source name, date — URL**.

Every bullet starts with a verification tag:

- **[P]** primary or official document, opened and read in this session (law text, parliamentary paper, ministry or agency publication, court-related release, official statistic).
- **[R]** reputable secondary source opened and read in this session (news agency, quality press, trade press, professional association).
- **[S]** seen only as a search-result snippet; the page itself was not opened or was blocked. Lower confidence. Re-check before use.
- **[A]** analyst-firm or vendor claim. Marketing grade, methodology not public.
- **UNVERIFIED** no primary or reputable source could be confirmed; the bullet says what was found instead.

Notes on quotes:

- German, Spanish and English quotes are given in the original language.
- Quotes marked *(fragment)* were returned by the fetch tool in shortened form. Read the full sentence at the URL before printing it.
- Where a source contradicts another, both values are listed. Nothing has been averaged or rounded.

Limit of this research: the session's web-search allowance ran out before every item could be double-checked. The items that still need a check are listed in the last section, "Open items".

### 1.4 Los Angeles wildfires (Eaton and Palisades), USA, January 2025

- [R] Deaths — the fires "led to the deaths of 31 people" — LAist, 25 Sept 2025 — https://laist.com/news/climate-environment/l-a-county-report-about-eaton-and-palisades-fires-finds-failings-in-emergency-response
- [P] Losses — "Overall losses totalled around US$ 53bn, including insured losses of roughly US$ 40bn."; costliest natural disaster of 2025 — Munich Re, 13 Jan 2026 — https://www.munichre.com/en/company/media-relations/media-information-and-corporate-news/media-information/2026/natural-disaster-figures-2025.html
- [P] No shared real-time information — "First responders and incident commanders were unable to consistently share real-time information due to unreliable cellular connectivity, inconsistent field reporting methods, and the use of various unconnected platforms." — County of Los Angeles, "McChrystal After-Action Report: Alerts, Warnings and Evacuations. Highlights of Findings and Recommendations", 25 Sept 2025 — https://file.lacounty.gov/SDSInter/lac/1192778_AAR_HighlightsandRecommendations.pdf
- [P] Loss of the aerial picture — "The extreme and rapidly moving fire conditions challenged the situational awareness of fire and law enforcement first responders, making it difficult to communicate the fire's location to the public. This was especially prevalent during the Eaton Fire, when wind conditions grounded aerial resources, including surveillance, almost immediately after the fire started." — County of Los Angeles, highlights of the McChrystal report, 25 Sept 2025 — URL as above
- [P] The report's fourth focus area — "Situational awareness and interoperability are pivotal areas requiring immediate enhancement. ## Ten strongest facts for a pitch

Chosen for the quality of the source first and the force of the fact second.

1. **The costliest single event in post-war Germany was a picture problem as much as a water problem.** At least 135 dead in the Ahr valley; total damage "schätzungsweise 40,5 Mrd. €". Sources: dpa via Deutsches Ärzteblatt, 2 Aug 2024; Prognos for BMWK, 29 June 2022.
2. **The video existed and nobody could use it.** A police helicopter filmed the valley from 22.14 to 22.43 Uhr, including people signalling with lamps from roofs. The videos were "nicht ordnungsgemäß verbucht", and until September 2022 the inquiry and the prosecutors assumed that no such recordings existed. The inquiry report speaks of a "defizitäres Lagebild". Sources: dpa via Volksstimme, 5 Oct 2022; epd, 4 Oct 2022; dpa via Deutsches Ärzteblatt, 2 Aug 2024.
3. **The crisis staff was blind.** Prosecutors: "So wusste die Technische Einsatzleitung nicht, was im Laufe des Abends noch auf sie zukommen würde." The criminal file holds "etwa 16.000 Blätter" of main files plus "etwa 19.000 Blätter" of further files, and the review took "etwa viereinviertel Jahre". Sources: beck-aktuell, 18 April 2024; Generalstaatsanwaltschaft Koblenz, Oct 2025.
4. **Valencia: the data was there, the alert came after the water.** 237 dead. The mobile alert went out at 20.11, "cuando ya se habían producido las inundaciones". The river gauge had shown 2.282 m3/s at 18:55 before it was swept away, and 19.821 emergency calls had come in. Sources: La Moncloa, 29 Oct 2025; investigating judge via Europa Press, 10 March 2025; MITECO, 4 Nov 2024.
5. **Los Angeles County's own review names unconnected systems.** "First responders and incident commanders were unable to consistently share real-time information due to [...] the use of various unconnected platforms." 31 dead; about US$ 53bn overall and US$ 40bn insured losses. Sources: County of Los Angeles (McChrystal report highlights), 25 Sept 2025; LAist, 25 Sept 2025; Munich Re, 13 Jan 2026.
6. **Texas: three hours and 21 minutes of warning, no county alert.** First federal flash flood warning at 1:14 a.m.; Kerr County "did not send any wireless alerts through IPAWS on July 4"; at least 135 dead. Sources: Texas Tribune, 8 and 31 July 2025; NBC 5 Investigates, 10 July 2025; CBS News, 31 July 2025.
7. **Maui: 850 gigabytes of video and audio, released afterwards.** At least 102 dead; the official analysis found a "siloed command structure"; the state later published "approximately 850 gigabytes of video files, audio files, photographs and documents", including police body camera footage. Sources: Hawaiʻi Attorney General, 13 Sept 2024; Maui Now, 13 Sept 2024.
8. **The German government has written the product requirement itself.** Ten billion euros for civil protection by 2029, and: "Der Bund entwickelt mit den Ländern ein gemeinsames digitales Lagebild Inland für den Bevölkerungsschutz [...] digital und in Echtzeit." Status in July 2026: "wird derzeit erprobt und konsolidiert". Sources: Federal Ministry of the Interior, Eckpunkte, 20 May 2026; Bundestag Drucksache 21/7126, 9 July 2026.
9. **The money is constitutionally protected and rising.** Since March 2025 the debt brake exempts spending above 1% of GDP on defence, "Zivil- und Bevölkerungsschutz sowie für die Nachrichtendienste". The BBK budget is planned to rise from 332.761.000 € (2025) to 816.381.000 € (2029). Sources: Grundgesetz Art. 115 Abs. 2; Federal Ministry of the Interior, Statusbericht, 2 April 2026.
10. **The losses are large, rising and mostly uninsured.** EU: around EUR 920 billion from 1980 to 2025, over EUR 270 billion of it in the last five years, less than 20% insured. World 2025: US$ 224bn overall, US$ 108bn insured. Sources: European Environment Agency, 16 Nov 2026; Munich Re, 13 Jan 2026.

Worth a slide of its own for the go-to-market: 400 districts and independent cities are the lower disaster-control authorities (294 Landkreise, 106 kreisfreie Städte; Federal Statistical Office via Deutscher Städtetag), and the federal government told parliament it has no data on the number of control centres (Bundestag Drucksache 19/27556).
