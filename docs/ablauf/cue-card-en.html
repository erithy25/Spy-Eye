Cue card for the demonstration · 13 minutes

What to do, what to say.

One page for the lectern, with the console switched to English (EN, top right). Ledger entries, event titles and source names in search hits stay German. The full run-of-show is in docs/DEMO.md (German).

Before

  • The evening before, online: .\vorfuehrung.cmd -NurPruefen. The command ends with “Bereit”.
  • On the day: close the windows of earlier runs, then .\vorfuehrung.cmd. The browser opens by itself.
  • Chrome or Edge, full screen (F11), zoom 100 %.
  • Sign in: leitung, password lagebild, mandate “Exercise RHEIN-MOSEL”. Below it says “31 recordings ready”.
  • Second window: 127.0.0.1:8788 (Trier), also leitung, tab LEDGER.
  • Clock at 4×: ten minutes of exercise in two and a half. PAUSE stops clock and sources.

Numbers to quote

  • Exercise in real time: 13 of 13 events, 15 alerts, one false alert, delay 1.1 s at the median.
  • Search on general footage: 54 of 66 queries answered.
  • 44 recordings searchable 8 s after the upload began.
  • Analysis at 3.3 times real time, four cores, no graphics card.
  • 88 automated checks; the whole path also runs on a Windows machine.
  • Code review: 21 findings, the severe ones fixed. No penetration test.

If something hangs

  • “Anschlüsse belegt” (ports taken): end the other window with Ctrl+C, repeat the command.
  • No false alert at the lorry: the rehearsal was not reset. Ctrl+C, .\vorfuehrung.cmd.
  • Federation “not reachable”: wait a few seconds.
  • Unsure: second terminal, pnpm demo:check.
  • Nothing works: show the product film (137 s, English cards over the German console).

Do not claim
